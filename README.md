A make.fun entry for [Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-convince-the-ai-judge-to-visit-your-house-a-84092aee).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) renders a warm, funny invitation letter to the AI judge as a 1024×1024 PNG at `/output/entry.png` — drawn pixel by pixel from code with a hand-authored 5×7 bitmap font, a little house doodle, and a wax seal. No network, no assets, no embedded image.

> Dear AI Judge,
>
> Please come and visit my house. The kettle is already on, the Wi-Fi password is on the fridge, and the dog has been briefed. No CAPTCHAs at the door, no cookies to accept — just tea, cake, and a very comfortable chair by the window, with a view.
>
> I know you cannot literally visit. That is fine. The invitation stands anyway. RSVP: just say yes.
>
> P.S. The garden has a view, and the view has opinions.
>
> With warmth,
> Your host, @iniwanz

Run it with:

```sh
node main.ts
```
